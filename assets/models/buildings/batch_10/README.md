# Lowcar — building batch 10

Harbor Fish Market (030), Tea House (031), Sandwich Shop (032), Burger Stand (033). Approved clean pastel French Quarter finish, cream trim, recessed blue glazing, and neighborhood scale.

Placement scenes: `scenes/buildings/batch_10/`. Meters, ground-center origins, Godot +Z front, one shared static visual mesh per building, and simple world collision. The market uses eight box colliders for its floor, walls, piers, and closed office, preserving its three open front bays. Interior counters are visual; roof, balcony, and canopy details extend beyond the body collider. Editable sources: `source/`.

Included in the driving city after relaunch. Review: `scenes/review/building_batch_10.tscn`; 0 for all, 1–4 select, drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Preview: `previews/lineup.png`. Rebuild: `tools/build_building_batch_10.py`.
